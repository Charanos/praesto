Data notes

Read the evidence in context.

This dashboard compares collected pharmacy catalogue listings in Kenya. It helps you inspect reported stock, listed prices and source coverage.

A snapshot, not live inventory

Stock and prices describe listings at collection. A source may have changed since then. Confirm current stock, pack, strength, form and price with the pharmacy before visiting or ordering.

When collection dates are inferred from file timestamps, the dashboard marks them as inferred. A file date does not verify when the pharmacy last updated a listing.

Missing stock signals stay unknown

Some feeds do not publish a usable out-of-stock signal. Their listings remain searchable, but they are excluded from the headline availability rate. “Stock not reported” does not mean in stock, out of stock or not listed.

A product can have several source listings with differing stock reports. Open its detail to read each listing. Cross-source stockouts do not establish a national shortage.

Product grouping has limits

Listings are grouped using product identity, pack, strength and form evidence. Match confidence describes that grouping. It does not establish that two products are clinically interchangeable.

Listed prices can refer to differing packs or source descriptions. Inspect the original pharmacy listing before comparing them as equivalent offers.

Reference coverage is incomplete

Reference-linked ingredients and supplied indications are an extract from the local reference data. Linkage does not guarantee a complete or current clinical monograph. Missing indications, interactions, precautions or pregnancy information do not establish absence of a risk.

Detailed clinical text requires a licensed source integration. This dashboard does not select a treatment or a personalized dose.

Search and exports

Search runs in your browser after the catalogue loads. Queries and filters appear in the page URL so a selection can be shared. A copied link includes those terms. No third-party analytics has been added by this revamp.

CSV exports retain the analytical snapshot’s existing field definitions. Raw product aggregate fields can differ from judged source-level evidence. Use the listing detail and reporting-source denominator to interpret availability.
